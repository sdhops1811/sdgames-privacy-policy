# SDGames Privacy Policy

Privacy policy website for **Even Up: Number Logic Puzzle**, published by **SDGames**.

## Publish

1. Open repository **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Choose **main** and **/(root)**, then **Save**.
4. Visit https://sdhops1811.github.io/sdgames-privacy-policy/ once the Pages deployment completes.

## Before submitting to Google Play

The current HTML is a draft with an explicit SDK-verification notice. Verify the final Unity build's analytics, diagnostics, attribution, AppLovin MAX mediation networks, their privacy policies and data handling; confirm retention and deletion and update the public policy accordingly. Verify the Game's Play Console Data safety answers and in-app privacy link match the build.
